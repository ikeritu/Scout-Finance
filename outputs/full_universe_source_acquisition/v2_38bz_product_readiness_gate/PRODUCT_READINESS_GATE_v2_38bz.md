# Product Readiness Gate v2.38BZ

Decision: `PRODUCT_READINESS_PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS`.

The `Ranking global (experimental)` surface is usable as a local research screen with explicit limitations. It is not a recommendation engine, not a predictive product, not a price-target workflow, and not a broker workflow.

Verified real populations:

- Main ranking: 318
- Partial comparability: 373
- Review required: 124
- Blocked: 270
- Not yet scored: 26
- Total: 1,111

Required product surface is present: metrics, search, score/coverage filters, Top N, filtered CSV export, quick detail, separate populations, visible blocked assets, and explicit non-advice copy.

The gate remains limited by incomplete country coverage, missing adapters, blocked low-coverage assets, financial institutions needing a separate factor contract, price coverage gaps, and absence of out-of-sample predictive validation.

Next recommended phase: `v2.38CA -- Release Candidate / local packaging / operator guide`.
