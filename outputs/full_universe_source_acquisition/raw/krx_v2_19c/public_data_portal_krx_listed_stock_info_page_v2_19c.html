Details Information

  • Description
    KRX Listed Stock Information provides basic information on stocks listed on the Korea Exchange. The data is organized into a single operation, based on the base date or ISIN code. The data includes the base date, abbreviated code, ISIN code, market category, stock name, corporate registration number, and corporate name. ※ Data is updated daily. ※ All APIs provided by the Financial Services Commission are linked to data holders, collected, and then made public. Not all services provided by the Financial Services Commission are real-time. Data updates are made after 1:00 PM on the business day following the base date. For example, data for Fridays is provided on the following Monday. (If Monday is a public holiday, data is provided on the following business day.) ※ What is an ISIN? The International Securities Identification Number (ISIN) is a widely used standard securities code. It is a 12-digit unique number assigned to each securities product. This data can be used for various capital market-related analyses, such as listing stock analysis, providing investor information, understanding market conditions, and generating industry-specific statistics. ※ For foreign companies, a domestic corporate registration number is not provided. ※ A foreign company refers to a corporation established in a country other than the Republic of Korea and registered and operated in accordance with the laws of that country. If the company has not separately registered as a corporation in Korea, a domestic corporate registration number does not exist.
